ABOUT CELESTE VVONG

The Irregular as Regular

Established in 2026, CELESTE VVONG is a Kuala Lumpur-based design atelier that celebrates and reinvigorates their biggest source of inspiration — architectural youth culture. With values rooted in ethical production, tactile deconstruction, and a curiosity to subvert normativity, Celeste VVong’s creations pay homage to those who regard the irregular as their regular.
