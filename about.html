Brand Story

Established in 2026, CELESTE VVONG is a contemporary design label that celebrates and reinvigorates its biggest source of inspiration – modern youth culture and brutalist architecture. With values rooted in ethical production and a curiosity to subvert normativity, our creations pay homage to those who regard the irregular as their regular.

Values & Mission

We believe that fashion should be a canvas for unapologetic self-expression. By prioritizing sustainable practices and partnering with local artisans, we ensure that every garment not only makes a distinct visual impact but is built to last. Our mission is to empower individuals to wear their identity proudly.

The Designer

At the helm of the label is Celeste, whose multidisciplinary background informs the brand's structured yet fluid aesthetic. Blending harsh geometric lines with soft, organic fabrics, Celeste constantly challenges the boundaries of conventional streetwear, offering a unique perspective that bridges high fashion and everyday utility.

"To wear CELESTE VVONG is to embrace the beautiful contradictions of modern life."
— Celeste
